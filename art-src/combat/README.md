# Combat art

Every export in `assets/combat/` has a matching editable `.blend` here, generated by the Python scripts in this folder. Names use English lowercase snake_case (`unit_spearman`, `crest_lion`, `terrain_river_straight`, `tree_oak_autumn`, …). `combat_asset_manifest.json` lists file, triangles, budget, dimensions, materials, rigs, animations and the crest anchor height per unit.

## Build

Blender 5.2, then import in Godot:

```powershell
& 'C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe' --background --factory-startup --python art-src/combat/build_combat_assets.py
godot --headless --import
```

Without arguments the build exports the house-neutral units, the crests and the review catalogue. Groups after `--` select other sets, e.g. `… --python art-src/combat/build_combat_assets.py -- units scenery`:

- `units` — `unit_<role>.glb` and `crest_<house>.glb` plus `combat_asset_catalog.png`; a unit or crest over its triangle budget stops the build.
- `scenery` — terrain, trees, rocks, foliage, camp props and `weapon_spear`. The scenery scripts have drifted from the committed exports: a rebuild changes those files, so review the diff before committing it.

## Units

One house-neutral model per unit: `unit_levy`, `unit_spearman`, `unit_crossbowman`, `unit_brabanter`, `unit_knight`, `unit_leader`, `unit_trebuchet`. Figures are stocky, 1.90 m to the helmet top with the head (chin to helmet top) one fifth of that.

| Unit | Look | Attack clip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| levy | linen smock, hood with shoulder cape (Gugel), flail, no helmet | two wild flail swings |
| spearman | padded gambeson, kettle hat, long spear, small round shield | lowers the spear and thrusts |
| crossbowman | linen gambeson, mail coif, crossbow, big pavise on the back | kneels, shoots, spans with the foot |
| brabanter | mismatched mail, iron and leather pauldrons, nasal helm, battle axe, wide stance; no house colours | overhead chop |
| knight | mail, tabard, heater shield, great helm, lance, sheathed sword; chestnut horse with caparison | couched lance thrust |
| leader | knight plus cloak, gold circlet, sword in hand, own banner on the saddle, pale horse | sword chop; `cheer` raises the sword |
| trebuchet | cocked counterweight trebuchet with sling, wheels, pennant and four crew | crew haul, arm swings, stone flies, crew winch back |

Weapons are held in the right hand (Blender −X). The spearman's grip is at Blender (−0.39, −0.23, 0.95), glTF (−0.39, 0.95, 0.23).

**House colour masks.** Every surface that shows house colours uses `house_primary` (tabard and gambeson, shield field, caparison, pavise, cloak, banner, hoods, trebuchet pennant) or `house_secondary` (hems, collars, shield and pavise borders, chevron and pale charges, caparison hem, banner band). Both are pure white so the game can tint them.

**Palette.** All other surfaces of units and crests share twelve colours (sRGB; `unit_palette.py`):

**Crests.** `crest_lion` (gold, Welfen), `crest_eagle` (black, Staufer), `crest_antlers` (Wettiner), `crest_wings` (blue, Wittelsbacher): 0.6–0.7 m tall, 0.55–0.85 m wide, at most 300 triangles, pivot at the mounting point (bottom centre), UV2 = (0, 0) so they bob with the torso. They mount on the Empty `crest_anchor` at the helmet top of the spearman, crossbowman, knight and leader (rest pose, static node under the scene root; follow the `head` bone when playing skeletal clips). Levy, brabanter and trebuchet have no anchor.

## Rigs and clips

Rigid skinning, one bone per part. Every human uses the same armature definition (`rig_human`; trebuchet crew `rig_crew_1` … `rig_crew_4`): `root` → `pelvis` → `spine` → `chest` → `neck` → `head`; `chest` → `upper_arm.L/R` → `forearm.L/R` → `hand.L/R`; `pelvis` → `thigh.L/R` → `shin.L/R` → `foot.L/R` (`.L` is the figure's left, Blender +X). Riders keep the bones but their rest pose is seated and their `root` sits at the saddle. Knight and leader share `rig_horse`: `root` → `body` → `neck` → `head`, `body` → `tail`, `body` → `front_upper.L/R` → `front_lower.L/R`, `body` → `hind_upper.L/R` → `hind_lower.L/R`. The trebuchet frame uses `rig_trebuchet` (`root`, `axle_front`, `axle_back`, `arm` → `counterweight`, `arm` → `sling`, `stone`).

Clips at 24 fps, exported as named glTF animations covering every rig of the file: humans `idle`, `walk`, `attack`, `hit`, `death` (comic tumble), `cheer`; mounted units add `horse_trot`, `horse_gallop`, `horse_charge`, and their human clips move horse and rider together. Each rig keeps one NLA track per clip in its `.blend`; `unit_clips.py` and `unit_clips_mounted.py` define the clips as rotations on top of each rest pose.

## Runtime conventions

Blender uses Z up and −Y forward. glTF uses Y up and +Z forward, metres, ground-centred pivots. UV2 stores the GPU stride mask: x = stride weight (1 on legs, 0 elsewhere; riders' legs stay 0), y = left/right phase. It must not be repurposed for lightmaps. The renderer batches the rest mesh in a MultiMesh and animates limbs from UV2; it skips the spearman's `weapon_spear` child and draws `weapon_spear.glb` separately.

## Previews

`combat_asset_catalog.png` (`combat_asset_catalog.blend`) shows every unit twice, tinted for the Welfen (red/gold, lion) and the Wittelsbacher (blue/silver, wings) with the crest mounted, the trebuchet from the side, and the four crests at twice their size in front. The tints exist only in the catalogue. `combat_battle_preview.png` and `combat_unit_preview.png` show the prototype; `landscape_summer_meadow.png`, `landscape_river_valley.png`, `landscape_autumn_woodland.png` the landscape compositions.

Run `godot res://src/render/battle/combat_art_preview.tscn` to inspect the moving landscapes; keys 1–3 switch presets. The compact preview stages a central river and bridge without running combat. The real prototype keeps its decorative river outside the playable area: traversable river terrain and bridge pathfinding are not implemented by this art change.

`data/combat_art.json` controls palettes, density, water and wind. The matching shader files live under `src/render/battle/`.
